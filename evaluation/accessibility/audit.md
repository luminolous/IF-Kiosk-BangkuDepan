# Accessibility audit

At least **six** checks on your facet, of which at least **three are measured in the lobby** (a tape measure,
a phone camera, a contrast checker on a photo or screenshot, a stopwatch), not judged by eye.
The checklist K1 to K10 and how to measure each one is in the **Field Kit** on myITS Classroom.
You may add checks of your own; give them codes K11 onwards.

Put photos, screenshots and simulations in `evidence/` and name them by check, e.g. `K2-label-at-2m.jpg`.
**No faces and no people** in any photo.
