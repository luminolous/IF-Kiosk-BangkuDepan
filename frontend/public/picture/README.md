# Lecturer photos

The official kiosk shows a photo for each lecturer, named `<kode>.webp` (for example `HF.webp`).

They are left out of this course copy on purpose: your prototype is published on a public site, and the lecturers agreed to appear on the official kiosk, not on ten student copies. Without a photo the kiosk shows the lecturer's initials, and `npm run data:check` prints a warning per lecturer. That warning is expected here and does not break anything.

Do not add photos of real people to this folder.
